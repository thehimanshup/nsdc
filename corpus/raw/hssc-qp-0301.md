## Qualification Overview
The Phlebotomy Technician qualification (HSS/Q0301) by HSSC prepares
candidates to collect, label, store and transport blood specimens in
laboratory and hospital settings. Aligned to NSQF Level 4.

## Eligibility
Minimum educational qualification: Class 12 pass with science subjects
preferred; Class 10 pass with relevant bridge training where scheme rules
permit. Minimum age: 17 years.

## Occupational Standards (NOS)
HSS/N0301 — Collect blood specimens safely using venipuncture and capillary
techniques.
HSS/N0302 — Label, store and transport specimens maintaining chain of custody.

## Training Duration
Indicative duration approximately 360 hours including laboratory practice.

## Career Progression
Phlebotomy Technicians work in diagnostic laboratories, hospitals and
collection centres, progressing toward senior lab-technician roles with
further qualifications.
