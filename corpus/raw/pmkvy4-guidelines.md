## Objective
Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0 (PMKVY 4.0) enables Indian youth to
take up industry-relevant NSQF-aligned skill training free of cost, covering
Short Term Training (STT), Special Projects and Recognition of Prior
Learning (RPL).

## Eligibility Criteria
An applicant must be an Indian national. The applicable age band for
short-term training is 15 to 45 years. A valid Aadhaar and an
Aadhaar-linked bank account are required for enrolment and certification.

## Training and Certification
Training is delivered at empanelled training centres by accredited training
providers. Candidates undergo assessment by designated assessment agencies
and receive certification upon successful demonstration of competency.
Training under the scheme is provided free of cost to eligible candidates.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card, bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked account), and educational
certificates as applicable to the chosen qualification.

## Support Provisions
The scheme includes provisions for placement facilitation support and
post-training handholding as notified by MSDE from time to time.
