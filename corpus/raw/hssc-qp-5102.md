## Qualification Overview
The Home Health Aide (HHA) qualification (HSS/Q5102) by HSSC prepares
candidates to deliver personal care and support services to patients in
home settings. Aligned to NSQF Level 3.

## Eligibility
Minimum educational qualification: Class 8 pass (Class 10 preferred).
Minimum age: 17 years.

## Occupational Standards (NOS)
HSS/N5121 — Provide home-based personal care to patients.
HSS/N5122 — Support mobility and comfort of patients at home.

## Training Duration
Indicative duration approximately 300 hours, typically delivered in blended
mode combining classroom and supervised home-care practice.

## Career Progression
Home Health Aides may progress to General Duty Assistant (HSS/Q5101, NSQF
Level 4) and further patient-care qualifications.
