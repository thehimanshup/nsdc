# Skilling FAQ (compiled extract)
> SEED DOCUMENT — compile from public NSDC/SIDH FAQ pages and re-verify.

## What is a Qualification Pack?
A Qualification Pack (QP) defines a job role through a set of National
Occupational Standards (NOS) with an assigned NSQF level.

## Is training under PMKVY free?
Yes — training under PMKVY 4.0 is provided free of cost to eligible
candidates at empanelled centres.

## Can I study in Hindi?
Training and assessment are available in Hindi and other regional languages
at many centres, subject to centre and content availability.

## What happens after certification?
Certified candidates receive digitally verifiable certificates and may
access placement facilitation support where provided under the scheme.
