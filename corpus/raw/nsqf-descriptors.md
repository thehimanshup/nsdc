## About NSQF
The National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) organises qualifications
in a series of levels from 1 to 10 based on professional knowledge,
professional skill, core skill and responsibility.

## Qualification Pack
A Qualification Pack (QP) is a set of National Occupational Standards (NOS)
aligned to a job role, assigned an NSQF level, and registered on the
National Qualification Register (NQR) after NCVET approval.

## Selected Level Descriptors
Level 3: Person may carry out a job which requires limited range of
activities, routine and predictable, with clear choices.
Level 4: Person may carry out work in familiar, predictable, routine
situations of clear choice, with some responsibility for own work within
defined limits.
Level 5: Person may carry out a job requiring well-developed skill, with
clear choice of procedures in familiar context, with responsibility for
own work and some supervision of others.
