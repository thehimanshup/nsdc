## Qualification Overview
The General Duty Assistant (GDA) qualification (HSS/Q5101), developed by the
Healthcare Sector Skill Council (HSSC), prepares candidates to provide
non-clinical patient care in hospitals and healthcare facilities. The
qualification is aligned to NSQF Level 4.

## Eligibility
Minimum educational qualification: Class 10 pass. Minimum age: 17 years
(candidates aged 15-17 may enrol under specific scheme provisions where
permitted). No prior work experience is required.

## Occupational Standards (NOS)
This Qualification Pack comprises the following National Occupational Standards:
HSS/N5101 — Assist patients with daily living activities.
HSS/N5102 — Monitor and record vital signs including temperature, pulse,
respiration and blood pressure.
HSS/N5104 — Maintain patient hygiene and follow infection control practices.
HSS/N5110 — Handle biomedical waste according to prescribed protocol.

## Training Duration
Indicative training duration is approximately 390 hours, covering theory,
practical laboratory work and on-the-job training components.

## Career Progression
Certified General Duty Assistants work in hospitals, nursing homes and
home-care settings. With experience and further NSQF-aligned qualifications,
progression paths include senior patient-care roles and supervisory positions.

## Assessment
Assessment covers each NOS with theory and practical components. A candidate
must demonstrate competency in all NOS units to be certified.
